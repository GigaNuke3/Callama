Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Callama is a free desktop app made by GigaNuke3 in the Philippines. This policy covers the Callama app and this website. The short version: the app collects nothing about you, and this website doesn't track you.

The Callama app

  • Your data stays on your computer. Chats, saved memories, your profile bio and settings are stored only in a local database (callama.db) in the app's data folder on your device. We never receive a copy.
  • No accounts, analytics or telemetry. The app does not send usage data, crash reports or any personal information anywhere.
  • The AI runs locally. Callama talks to Ollama on your own machine (localhost). Your messages are not sent to any online AI service.
  • Connections to Ollama's servers. If Ollama isn't installed, Callama downloads and runs Ollama's official installer from ollama.com. When you pull a model, Ollama downloads it from its own registry. Those requests are handled by Ollama under its own privacy policy and contain no chat content.
  • Deleting your data is in your hands: delete chats and memories in the app, or delete the app's data folder.

This website

  • We use no cookies, analytics or trackers.
  • The site is hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure its service.
  • Downloads are served by GitHub, which handles those requests under its own privacy statement.

Donations

Tips are handled entirely by PayPal. We only see what PayPal shows any payment recipient (typically your name, email and the amount) and use it only to keep records of the donation. We never see your card or bank details.

Children

Callama collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Donations through PayPal are subject to PayPal's own age requirements.

Your rights

We respect your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Because the app keeps everything on your device, you already control your data. For any data we may hold from a donation, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
